# Scenarios

The seven-phase lifecycle applies to every organization, but *how* you move through it depends heavily on what kind of organization you are. A five-person startup and a 200-zone regulated enterprise both do Strategy, Foundation, and Adopt — but they make very different decisions inside each phase.

This section applies the full framework to four common organizational profiles. Each scenario is a lens on the same seven phases, not a replacement for them — read the phase pages for the how, and a scenario page for the specific trade-offs that profile tends to hit.

| Scenario | Defining characteristic | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| [Startup](startup.md) | Small team, cost- and speed-sensitive, minimal governance overhead | One or two zones, no dedicated security team |
| [Agency & Multi-Tenant](agency-multi-tenant.md) | Managing many customers' Cloudflare zones under one relationship | Web agencies, MSPs, SaaS platforms provisioning per-customer zones |
| [Migrating from a Legacy Vendor](legacy-vendor-migration.md) | Replacing an existing CDN, WAF, or VPN vendor, not greenfield adoption | Contract-driven cutover with a hard deadline |
| [Regulated & Compliance-Heavy Orgs](regulated-industry.md) | Adoption gated by audit, data residency, or sector-specific compliance requirements | Finance, healthcare, government, or public sector |

If your organization doesn't cleanly match one profile, that's normal — most real organizations are a blend. Read the scenario closest to your situation, then pull in the individual phase pages for anything it points to.
